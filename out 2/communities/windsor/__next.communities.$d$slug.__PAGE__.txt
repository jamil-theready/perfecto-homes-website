3:Taf6,<h2>Windsor: Sonoma County Wine Country Living</h2>
<p>Windsor sits in the heart of Sonoma County, about 65 miles north of San Francisco. With a population around 27,000, it offers a rare combination: wine country lifestyle, small town feel, and enough proximity to Santa Rosa and San Francisco to stay connected.</p>
<p>The town is surrounded by vineyards. Russian River Valley appellations wrap around Windsor, making it one of the most accessible wine country communities in Northern California. People live here because they want the Sonoma life without the Healdsburg or Sonoma price tag.</p>
<h2>Why People Choose Windsor</h2>
<p>Wine country living at a price point that actually makes sense. While Healdsburg and Sonoma command premium prices, Windsor delivers the same rolling hills, vineyard views, and farm to table dining culture at a significant discount.</p>
<p>The community is family oriented with well maintained parks, organized youth sports, and a walkable town center called Windsor Town Green. Farmers markets, outdoor concerts, and community events happen regularly. It feels like a community that knows each other.</p>
<h2>Schools</h2>
<p>Windsor Unified School District serves the area and is well regarded in Sonoma County. Windsor High School is the primary public high school. Elementary and middle school options are spread across the town's neighborhoods. The district emphasizes small class sizes and college preparation.</p>
<h2>Real Estate</h2>
<p>Windsor home prices range from the mid $500,000s to over $1 million for larger properties with vineyard views. The market includes single family homes in planned communities, custom homes on larger lots, and the occasional property with acreage.</p>
<p>Compared to neighboring Healdsburg (median around $1.2M) and Sebastopol, Windsor offers substantial savings while keeping you in Sonoma County wine country.</p>
<h2>Getting Around</h2>
<p>Highway 101 runs through Windsor, connecting it to Santa Rosa (15 minutes south) and San Francisco (about 70 minutes without traffic). The Sonoma County Airport is right next door with limited commercial service. San Francisco International and Oakland International are both within 90 minutes.</p>
<h2>Local Highlights</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Russian River</strong> — Kayaking, canoeing, swimming in summer</li>
<li><strong>Windsor Town Green</strong> — Community events, farmers market, outdoor concerts</li>
<li><strong>Sonoma County Wineries</strong> — Dozens within a 15 minute drive</li>
<li><strong>Foothill Regional Park</strong> — Hiking trails with valley views</li>
<li><strong>Farm to Table Dining</strong> — Local restaurants sourcing from nearby farms</li>
</ul>
