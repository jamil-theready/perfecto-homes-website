3:T1108,<h2>Citrus Heights: A Sacramento Suburb That Delivers</h2>
<p>Citrus Heights sits just northeast of Sacramento, offering a suburban lifestyle without the long commute. The city covers about 14 square miles and is home to roughly 87,000 residents who enjoy tree lined streets, local parks, and easy access to shopping and dining along Sunrise Boulevard and Greenback Lane.</p>
<p>Families gravitate toward Citrus Heights for good reason. The cost of living runs lower than many neighboring communities, and the neighborhoods feel established. You get sidewalks, mature landscaping, and a genuine sense of community. Block parties still happen here. Neighbors still wave.</p>

Working with a local team that knows the market makes all the difference.</p>

<h3>Things to Do</h3>
<p>Sunrise Mall anchors the retail scene, but the real gems are the local spots. Rusch Park hosts community events throughout the year, and the Sunrise Recreation and Park District runs programs for kids, adults, and seniors. The Stock Ranch area offers quick access to restaurants and entertainment along the Sunrise Corridor.</p>
<p>Outdoor lovers appreciate the proximity to Folsom Lake and the American River Parkway. A short drive puts you on trails, kayaks, or picnic blankets along the river.</p>

<p>Most neighborhoods in Citrus Heights sit within a 10 minute drive of multiple school options. Walking distance to school is common in established neighborhoods near Sunrise and Greenback.</p>
<h3>Parks and Recreation</h3>
<p>Parks are everywhere. Rusch Park alone covers 50 acres with sports fields, a community pool, tennis courts, and a full community center. The Sunrise Recreation and Park District runs year round programs for all ages.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Rusch Park</strong> (50 acres, sports, pool, community center)</li>
<li><strong>Tempo Park</strong> (quiet green space, walking trails)</li>
<li><strong>Arcade Creek Park</strong> (creek trail, nature area)</li>

</ul>
