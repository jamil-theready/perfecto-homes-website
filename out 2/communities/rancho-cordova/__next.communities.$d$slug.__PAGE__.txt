<h3>Things to Do</h3>
<p>The American River runs along the northern border of Rancho Cordova, and residents take full advantage. Kayaking, fishing, and riverside picnics are weekend staples. The Mather Regional Park and Mather Field offer open space, trails, and community events.</p>
<p>Local dining has grown alongside the city. Restaurants and breweries along Folsom Boulevard serve a mix of cuisines. The annual Rancho Cordova Fourth of July celebration is one of the largest in the Sacramento region.</p>
<h3>Schools and Parks</h3>
<p>Folsom Cordova Unified School District serves most of Rancho Cordova. Schools like Williamson Elementary and Cordova High School serve the community well. Newer developments in the Sunridge area have brought additional schools to keep pace with growth.</p>
<p>Parks are a standout feature. Hagan Community Park spans over 75 acres and hosts festivals, sports leagues, and the community pool. Smaller neighborhood parks are woven throughout the residential areas.</p>
