Why Invest in Sacred Valley Real Estate

Elisban GonzalesReal Estate Specialist
February 28, 2026·Peru·4 min read

Quick Answer

The Sacred Valley offers some of the lowest property prices in any major tourism corridor worldwide, with land available for $20 to $80 per square meter, strong tourism growth from over 1.5 million annual visitors, and major upside from the upcoming Chinchero airport.

Key Takeaways

  • 1Sacred Valley land costs $20 to $80 per square meter compared to $150 to $300 plus in Tulum and $100 to $250 in Bali
  • 2Over 1.5 million international visitors pass through the Cusco to Machu Picchu corridor each year
  • 3The Chinchero airport will bring direct international flights and is the biggest catalyst for property value growth
  • 4Investment options include hospitality businesses, restaurants, development land, and residential properties
  • 5Foreigners can buy property in Peru with the same legal rights as local citizens

The Sacred Valley of Peru sits between two of South America's most powerful tourism magnets: Cusco and Machu Picchu. Over 1.5 million international visitors pass through this corridor every year. The region offers fertile land, mild climate, rich culture, and property prices that remain remarkably low by global standards. For investors looking at Peru real estate investment, the Sacred Valley deserves serious attention.

Tourism Growth That Keeps Climbing

Peru's tourism sector has grown steadily over the past two decades. Machu Picchu is consistently ranked among the top destinations on earth. The Peruvian government has invested heavily in promoting the country as a world class travel destination, and the results show.

The Sacred Valley benefits directly from this momentum. Towns like Ollantaytambo, Urubamba, Pisac, and Chinchero see daily flows of international tourists. Many visitors now choose to stay in the valley itself rather than basing themselves in Cusco. The altitude is lower and more comfortable. The scenery is stunning. And the pace is more relaxed.

This shift in traveler behavior creates sustained demand for accommodation, dining, and experiences. It also creates opportunity for investors who can supply what visitors want.

Affordable Entry Prices

Compare Sacred Valley property prices to other popular investment destinations and the gap is striking:

  • Tulum, Mexico: beachfront land sells for $150 to $300+ per square meter.

  • Bali, Indonesia: rice field plots in Ubud range from $100 to $250 per square meter.

  • Sacred Valley, Peru: agricultural and development land can still be found for $20 to $80 per square meter in many areas.

These are not comparable in every dimension. But the point stands. The Sacred Valley offers entry prices that are a fraction of markets with similar tourism appeal. For investors who want to maximize their position, that spread represents upside.

Operating businesses are also priced attractively. You can acquire a functioning hostal or restaurant in the Sacred Valley for what a modest condo costs in Playa del Carmen.

The Chinchero Airport Factor

The upcoming Chinchero International Airport will transform access to the Sacred Valley. Instead of flying into Cusco and driving over an hour, visitors will land directly in the valley. This is the single biggest catalyst for property value growth in the region.

History shows that new airport access consistently lifts real estate prices in tourism markets. The Sacred Valley is positioned for the same trajectory. Investors who buy before the airport opens stand to benefit the most from appreciation.

Types of Sacred Valley Investment

The best place to invest in Peru depends on your goals and capital. The Sacred Valley supports several investment models:

Hotels and Hostals

Small to mid size hospitality properties generate revenue from day one. Occupancy rates in the Sacred Valley are strong during peak season (May through October) and increasingly solid year round. Hostal Qhispicay in Ollantaytambo is an example of a turnkey hospitality investment currently available through Perfecto Homes.

Restaurants and Food Businesses

Peru's culinary reputation is global. Restaurants in the Sacred Valley serve both international tourists and a growing domestic travel market. Hatuchay Valle Restaurant in Urubamba offers an established brand and location in the center of the valley's commercial activity.

Land for Development

Purchasing land for future development or long term appreciation is the most capital efficient strategy. The Sacred Valley has parcels suited for boutique hotels, eco lodges, residential projects, and agricultural ventures. Predio Victoria is one such property worth reviewing.

Residential Properties

A growing number of foreign nationals are relocating to the Sacred Valley full time or part time. The cost of living is low. The community is international. And the quality of daily life is hard to match at this price point. Residential purchases serve both personal use and rental income potential.

Why Now Is the Right Time

Three factors converge to make this a strong entry point for Sacred Valley investment:

  1. Pre airport pricing. Property values have not yet priced in the impact of the Chinchero airport. Once flights begin, that changes fast.

  2. Growing global awareness. Peru is increasingly featured in international travel media, food publications, and investment guides. Demand is building.

  3. Favorable legal framework. Foreigners can buy property in Peru with the same rights as local citizens. The process is clear and well documented.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does property cost in the Sacred Valley?
Land ranges from $20 to $80 per square meter in many areas. Operating businesses like hostals and restaurants can be acquired for what a modest condo costs in popular Mexican resort towns.
Can foreigners buy real estate in Peru?
Yes. Foreigners can purchase property in Peru with the same rights as local citizens. The legal process is clear and well documented with no special restrictions on foreign ownership.
Why is now the right time to invest in the Sacred Valley?
Property values have not yet priced in the Chinchero airport impact. Combined with growing global awareness of Peru as a destination and a favorable legal framework, current prices represent a strong entry point.
