32:T1777,<p>El Dorado Hills sits in the foothills east of Sacramento, offering a lifestyle that blends natural beauty with suburban convenience. The community has earned a reputation for excellent schools, upscale neighborhoods, and easy access to Folsom Lake and the Sierra Nevada. If you are considering living in El Dorado Hills, here is a straightforward look at what the area delivers.</p>
<p><a href="/">Perfecto Homes</a> helps buyers find the right fit in El Dorado Hills and across the greater Sacramento region. Let us walk you through what makes this community stand out.</p>
<h3><strong>The El Dorado Hills Lifestyle</strong></h3>
<p>El Dorado Hills is not a city. It is an unincorporated community in El Dorado County, which gives it a distinct character compared to neighboring Folsom or Sacramento. The pace here is slower. Streets are clean. Trails wind through oak studded hillsides. Neighbors know each other.</p>
<p>Outdoor recreation is a core part of daily life. Folsom Lake is minutes away for boating, paddleboarding, and hiking. The community itself is laced with multi use trails that connect neighborhoods to parks, schools, and commercial centers. Families spend weekends at the pool, on the trails, or at one of the many youth sports leagues that operate locally.</p>
<h3><strong>El Dorado Hills Town Center</strong></h3>
<p>Town Center is the commercial and social hub of the community. It is an open air lifestyle center with restaurants, boutique shops, a movie theater, and a weekly farmers market during warmer months. You will find local favorites alongside national chains, all set against a backdrop of rolling hills and mature landscaping.</p>
<p>Town Center hosts seasonal events throughout the year, from outdoor concerts to holiday festivals. It functions as the community gathering spot and gives El Dorado Hills a small town feel despite its growing population.</p>
<h3><strong>Schools and Education</strong></h3>
<p>Schools are one of the top reasons families choose El Dorado Hills. The Buckeye Union School District serves the area and consistently ranks among the best in the Sacramento region. Elementary and middle schools in the district post test scores well above state averages.</p>
<p>High school students typically attend Oak Ridge High School in the El Dorado Union High School District. Oak Ridge offers strong academics, competitive athletics, and a wide range of extracurricular programs. Families who prioritize education will find the school options here difficult to beat.</p>
<h3><strong>Serrano: The Master Planned Community</strong></h3>
<p>Serrano is the largest and most well known neighborhood within El Dorado Hills. This master planned community features a private golf course, multiple pools, a fitness center, tennis courts, and miles of walking trails. Homes in Serrano range from mid sized family residences to large custom estates.</p>
<p>The Serrano HOA maintains common areas to a high standard. Monthly dues cover access to community amenities, landscaping of shared spaces, and organized social events. For buyers who want a resort style living experience with structure and upkeep, Serrano delivers.</p>
<h3><strong>El Dorado Hills Cost of Living</strong></h3>
<p>The El Dorado Hills cost of living runs higher than the Sacramento County average. Median home prices in the community typically range from the high $700,000s to well over $1 million depending on the neighborhood, lot size, and age of the home.</p>
<p>Property taxes in El Dorado County are comparable to Sacramento County rates, generally hovering around 1.1% of assessed value. Some newer developments carry Mello Roos or Community Facilities District fees that can add to the monthly cost. Groceries, dining, and services are in line with the broader Sacramento area.</p>
<p>For buyers relocating from the Bay Area or Southern California, El Dorado Hills represents strong value. You get more square footage, better schools, and a quieter lifestyle for significantly less than comparable neighborhoods in the coastal metros.</p>
<h3><strong>Housing Types and What to Expect</strong></h3>
<p>El Dorado Hills homes for sale cover a broad spectrum. You will find everything from three bedroom starter homes near Highway 50 to five bedroom estates on acre lots in the upper hillside neighborhoods.</p>
<ul>
<li>Entry level homes start in the low $600,000s for older construction near the commercial corridor</li>
<li>Mid range homes in established subdivisions typically list between $750,000 and $950,000</li>
<li>Custom and semi custom homes in Serrano and other premium areas often exceed $1.2 million</li>
</ul>
<p>Most homes in the area were built between 1990 and 2015. Construction quality is generally strong, with stucco exteriors, tile roofs, and open floor plans being common across neighborhoods.</p>
<h3><strong>Folsom Lake Access</strong></h3>
<p>Folsom Lake is one of the biggest lifestyle perks of living in El Dorado Hills. The Brown's Ravine entrance sits just minutes from most neighborhoods. Residents use the lake year round for kayaking, fishing, swimming, and trail running along the shoreline.</p>
<p>The American River trail system also connects to Folsom Lake, giving cyclists and runners a network that stretches all the way into downtown Sacramento. If outdoor access matters to you, few communities in the region can compete.</p>
<h3><strong>Is El Dorado Hills Right for You?</strong></h3>
<p>Living in El Dorado Hills works best for buyers who value top schools, outdoor recreation, and a community oriented atmosphere. It is quieter than Sacramento proper and more upscale than many of the suburban alternatives in the region.</p>
