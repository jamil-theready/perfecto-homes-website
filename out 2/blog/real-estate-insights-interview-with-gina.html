Real Estate Insights: Interview with Gina

Gina GonzalezReal Estate Specialist
February 28, 2026·News·3 min read

Quick Answer

The best real estate investment opportunities in California include multi family properties generating 15% to 20% rental returns, home flipping with potential 20% annual returns from two flips per year, and single family homes in affordable markets like Sacramento.

Key Takeaways

  • 1Home flipping accounted for 9.6% of total homes sold in Q1 2022 with a gross return of 25.8%
  • 2Multi family properties like duplexes and fourplexes generate 15% to 20% rental income returns according to Gina Gonzalez
  • 3Flipping twice a year with a 10% profit margin per flip can yield a 20% annual return on investment
  • 4Sacramento offers affordability, strong demand, and proximity to major cities making it a real estate hotspot
  • 5Risks include renovation delays, rising property taxes, and tax reassessments that can cause buyers to walk away from deals

Investing in real estate, whether buying a home to live in or for resale/rental income, is one of the safest and most profitable ventures. Here, we’ll discuss key trends and opportunities that are thriving in today’s market, offering potential for significant returns.

Home Flipping: A Growing Trend

One of the fastest-growing opportunities in real estate is home flipping. This process involves purchasing older or distressed properties, renovating them, and selling them for profit. Flipping is an attractive option due to the limited inventory of new homes and the rising costs of construction.

Why Home Flipping Works

According to real estate expert Luis Ramírez, the cost of a new home per square meter is often double that of older homes, making flips more accessible for investors. For the first quarter of 2022, flipping accounted for 9.6% of total homes sold, the highest rate since 2000, compared to 6.9% in the previous quarter. However, the gross return on flipping dropped slightly to 25.8% from 27.3% in the previous quarter.

Additionally, the median price for flipped homes reached a record high, with a 10.5% increase compared to the last quarter of 2021.

Maximizing Returns with Flipping

Ramírez suggests that if your flipping investment (purchase cost + renovation budget) yields a minimum profit margin of 10%, you can achieve a 20% annual return by flipping twice a year.

Risks of Home Flipping

While flipping is lucrative, it carries risks:

  1. Delays in Renovations
    Extended remodeling timelines or permit approvals can impact profitability. Researching local timelines for renovation permits is crucial.

  2. Tax Increases
    Rising property taxes can cut into profits. If the home isn’t sold within six months, annual taxes could significantly impact returns. Additionally, buyers tend to avoid high-tax areas, potentially cooling the market.

An example is Austin, Texas, where increased taxes have led buyers to pull out of deals. Realtor Crystal López of Redfin mentioned a case where a tax reassessment doubled the property tax, causing the buyer to walk away from a $550,000 investment deal.

California Real Estate Opportunities

In an interview with Gina González, a California-based real estate expert, she outlined several key trends in the state’s housing market.

Popular Investment Types

  • New Construction Homes: Despite a need for new housing, construction companies have inventory available for buyers seeking brand-new properties.

  • Single-Family Homes: These are in high demand for average families, particularly in areas like Sacramento. Typical homes feature three bedrooms and two bathrooms.

  • Multi-Family Properties: Duplexes, triplexes, and fourplexes are highly sought after by investors.

Multi-Family Properties: The Golden Opportunity

According to Gina, multi-family units offer the greatest investment potential in today’s market.

“Rental income from multi-family homes generates returns between 15-20%, significantly higher than returns from single-family homes,” Gina explained. Many investors purchase multi-family properties, live in one unit, and rent out the others. Over time, these properties appreciate in value while providing consistent cash flow.

Why Sacramento?

Sacramento is an emerging hotspot for real estate investment, offering affordability and growth potential.

  • Affordability: The cost of living in Sacramento is significantly lower than in major California cities like San Francisco and Los Angeles.

  • Demand: With families seeking affordable single-family homes and investors eyeing multi-family properties, the market remains strong.

  • Proximity to Major Cities: Sacramento’s central location and access to transportation hubs, including an international airport, add to its appeal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best type of real estate investment in California right now?
Multi family properties offer the greatest potential. Rental income from duplexes, triplexes, and fourplexes generates 15% to 20% returns. Many investors live in one unit and rent the others, building equity while collecting consistent cash flow over time.
Is home flipping still profitable in California?
Yes. Home flipping hit 9.6% of total sales in Q1 2022 with median flip prices reaching record highs. A 10% profit margin per flip done twice a year can yield 20% annual returns. But watch for renovation delays and rising property taxes.
Why is Sacramento a good market for real estate investment?
Sacramento is significantly more affordable than San Francisco and Los Angeles while offering strong demand for both single family homes and multi family properties. Its central location, international airport, and growing population add to its investment appeal.
