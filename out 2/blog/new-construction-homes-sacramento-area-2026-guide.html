New Construction Homes in the Sacramento Area: Your 2026 Guide

Elisban GonzalesReal Estate Specialist
February 28, 2026·Sacramento·4 min read

Quick Answer

New construction homes in the Sacramento area are available in Elk Grove, Roseville, and Citrus Heights from builders like Lennar and Taylor Morrison. Expect modern layouts, energy efficient features, builder warranties, and a build timeline of 6 to 12 months.

Key Takeaways

  • 1Elk Grove new construction starts around $2,900 per month and includes entry level to estate style homes near the planned Civic Center
  • 2West Roseville and Highway 65 corridor developments feature solar panels, smart home tech, and open concept designs as standard
  • 3Citrus Heights offers the most affordable new construction entry point with infill developments close to downtown Sacramento
  • 4Negotiate upgrades rather than base price because builders typically have more flexibility on included features
  • 5Always bring your own agent to new construction purchases since the model home sales agent represents the builder

The Sacramento region is one of the fastest growing housing markets in California. Builders are responding to demand with new master planned communities, modern floor plans, and energy efficient designs across the metro area. If you are looking at new construction homes in Roseville, Elk Grove, or Citrus Heights, 2026 is shaping up to be a strong year for buyers.

At Perfecto Homes, we help families navigate the new construction process from start to finish. Here is what you need to know about buying a brand new home in the Sacramento area this year.

Why New Construction Is Gaining Momentum in Sacramento

Sacramento continues to attract buyers from the Bay Area and Southern California who want more space for less money. That migration trend, combined with local job growth in healthcare, tech, and state government, has pushed builders to break ground on thousands of new units across the region.

New construction homes offer several advantages over resale properties. You get modern layouts, updated building codes, energy efficient appliances, and builder warranties that cover major systems for years. Many new communities also include parks, trails, and community centers that add long term value.

Elk Grove: New Homes With a Family Focus

Elk Grove has become one of the top destinations for new construction in the Sacramento area. Builders like Lennar, Taylor Morrison, and Tri Pointe are actively developing neighborhoods in the southern portion of the city, near the planned Elk Grove Civic Center.

New homes in Elk Grove, CA range from entry level single family residences to larger estate style properties. Monthly costs for Elk Grove new construction typically start around $2,900 per month depending on loan terms and purchase price. The city's top rated schools, low crime rates, and diverse dining scene make it a natural fit for growing families.

Explore available listings in Elk Grove through Perfecto Homes.

Roseville: Strong Schools and Steady Growth

New construction homes in Roseville remain in high demand. The city consistently ranks among the best places to live in Northern California, thanks to its excellent school districts, clean streetscapes, and access to outdoor recreation at Folsom Lake.

Builders in Roseville are focused on communities in West Roseville and along the Highway 65 corridor. Expect to find open concept designs, solar panel packages, and smart home technology as standard features in many of these new builds. Pricing varies by neighborhood, but Roseville offers solid value compared to similar quality construction in the Bay Area.

Browse new homes and resale properties in Roseville with our team.

Citrus Heights: Affordable New Construction Close to Everything

Citrus Heights is gaining attention from buyers who want to stay close to downtown Sacramento without the higher price tags of neighboring communities. New homes in Citrus Heights, CA are popping up in infill developments and smaller boutique communities that blend into established neighborhoods.

The city sits near Interstate 80 and Highway 50, giving residents easy commuter access to Roseville, Folsom, and downtown Sacramento. For buyers on a tighter budget, Citrus Heights offers one of the best entry points into new construction ownership in the greater Sacramento market.

See what is available in Citrus Heights today.

What to Watch for When Buying New Construction

Buying a new build is different from purchasing a resale home. Here are a few things to keep in mind.

  • Negotiate upgrades, not just price. Builders often have more flexibility on included features than on the base price.

  • Get your own representation. The sales agent at the model home works for the builder. Having your own agent protects your interests.

  • Understand the timeline. New construction can take 6 to 12 months depending on the phase. Lock in your rate at the right time.

  • Review HOA details carefully. Many new communities include HOA fees that cover amenities and landscaping. Know what you are signing up for.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to buy a new construction home in Sacramento?
New construction homes in Sacramento typically take 6 to 12 months from contract to completion depending on the build phase. Locking in your mortgage rate at the right time during that window is important.
Where are new homes being built in Sacramento in 2026?
Builders are actively developing in southern Elk Grove near the Civic Center, West Roseville along Highway 65, and infill sites in Citrus Heights. Lennar, Taylor Morrison, and Tri Pointe are among the active builders.
Should I use my own agent when buying new construction?
Bringing your own buyer agent is strongly recommended. The sales agent at the model home works for the builder, not you. Your own agent negotiates on your behalf and protects your interests throughout the process.
