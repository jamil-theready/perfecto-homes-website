About This Property

A 3 bedroom, 3 bathroom single family home in Marysville listed at $635,000. This property stands out with a full bathroom for every bedroom, a feature that is hard to find at this price point.

Marysville sits in the Yuba Sutter region just north of Sacramento. The area offers a quieter pace of life without sacrificing access to city amenities. You get more land, more space, and more value for your dollar compared to properties closer to downtown Sacramento. It is one of the best kept secrets in the greater Sacramento market.

Three bedrooms give you flexibility for family living, a home office, or rental potential. Three full bathrooms mean no one is waiting in line. The layout is practical and move in ready, designed for everyday comfort.

This listing is already pending, which tells you everything about demand in this area. Marysville has seen steady growth as more buyers discover the value and quality of life it offers. Properties like this do not sit on the market for long.
