## Sacramento: California's Capital City Has It All

Sacramento is the beating heart of Northern California. With a metro population over two million, the city blends urban energy with a surprising amount of green space and neighborhood charm. From the historic grid downtown to the sprawling suburbs, Sacramento area real estate offers something for every buyer.

The farm to fork capital lives up to its name. World class dining, craft breweries, and weekend farmers markets define the local food scene. Add in professional sports, a revitalized downtown, and easy access to both the mountains and the coast, and you start to see why people keep moving here.

### Why Families Choose Sacramento

Families love Sacramento for the space. Compared to the Bay Area, your dollar stretches further here. Yards are bigger. Commutes are shorter. And the neighborhoods each carry their own personality. Midtown brings walkable culture. Land Park offers tree canopied streets and proximity to the zoo. Natomas delivers newer construction and family friendly parks.

The diversity of the city is a major draw. Sacramento consistently ranks as one of the most diverse cities in the country. That diversity shows up in the food, the festivals, and the public schools.

### Things to Do

The American River Parkway stretches 32 miles through the region, offering trails, fishing, and rafting. Old Sacramento brings history and riverfront dining. Golden 1 Center hosts concerts and Kings games year round. The Sacramento Zoo, Fairytale Town, and Funderland keep younger kids entertained for hours.

Weekend trips to Lake Tahoe take about 90 minutes. San Francisco is under two hours west. Sacramento sits right in the middle of everything.

### Schools

Sacramento is served by multiple school districts, including Sacramento City Unified, Elk Grove Unified, and Natomas Unified. That means families have a huge selection of campuses across every level.

**Elementary Schools**

- Sutterville Elementary. A Land Park staple with strong parent involvement and consistent test scores.
- Crocker Riverside Elementary. Located in the Pocket area. Known for its tight knit community and STEM offerings.
- Phoebe Hearst Elementary. One of the top rated elementaries in Midtown Sacramento.
- Matsuyama Elementary. Serves the Natomas area with dual language immersion and a dedicated staff.
- Theodore Judah Elementary. East Sacramento families love this school for its walkability and community feel.

**Middle Schools**

- California Middle School. A Sacramento City Unified campus in East Sacramento with strong academics and athletics.
- Sutter Middle School. Located in East Sacramento. Offers honors classes and a well rounded extracurricular program.
- Sam Brannan Middle School. Serves the South Sacramento area with career tech and college prep courses.
- Albert Einstein Middle School. A solid campus in the Arden Arcade area with active parent groups.
- Rosa Parks K through 8. A smaller campus model that keeps kids in one community through middle school.

**High Schools**

- C.K. McClatchy High School. One of the oldest and most respected high schools in the city. Strong AP program.
- John F. Kennedy High School. Serves the Pocket and South Land Park neighborhoods. Known for arts and athletics.
- Mira Loma High School. Houses the International Baccalaureate program. One of the top public high schools in the region.
- Natomas High School. A newer campus serving the growing Natomas community with modern facilities.
- Inderkum High School. Another strong Natomas Unified campus. Competitive athletics and college prep focus.

**Private and Charter Schools**

- Sacramento Country Day School. A respected K through 12 private school near Land Park.
- Christian Brothers High School. A well known Catholic high school with a strong alumni network.
- St. Francis High School. College prep private school on the east side of the city.
- Sacramento Charter High School. Offers a public charter option with smaller class sizes.
- Capitol Collegiate Academy. A high performing charter school serving K through 8 students downtown.

Many Sacramento neighborhoods offer walkable access to at least one school campus. Proximity to schools varies by neighborhood, so buyers should ask about specific attendance boundaries before making an offer.

### Parks

William Land Park anchors the south side of the city with 166 acres of green space, a golf course, and the Sacramento Zoo. McKinley Park in East Sacramento draws families with its pool, rose garden, and shaded playgrounds. River Bend Park along the American River Parkway gives residents direct access to trails and water recreation.

Southside Park downtown offers open fields and a lake within walking distance of the Capitol. Curtis Park is a neighborhood gem with a circle trail popular with joggers and dog walkers. Sacramento maintains over 230 parks across the city, so no matter where you live, green space is close.
