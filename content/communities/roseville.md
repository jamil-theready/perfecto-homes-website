## Roseville: Sacramento's Most In Demand Suburb

Roseville sits at the top of nearly every "best places to live" list in the Sacramento region. The city delivers excellent schools, safe neighborhoods, abundant shopping, and a quality of life that keeps families coming back generation after generation. It is no surprise that homes for sale in Roseville CA remain among the most sought after in Northern California.

Located along Interstate 80 in Placer County, Roseville offers easy commutes to Sacramento, access to Sierra Nevada recreation, and a local economy anchored by major employers like Adventist Health, Hewlett Packard Enterprise, and the city itself.

### Why Families Love Roseville

Schools are the number one reason families move to Roseville. The Roseville City School District and the Roseville Joint Union High School District consistently earn high marks. Top rated campuses like Olympus Junior High and Granite Bay High School draw families from across the region.

Beyond schools, the city runs one of the best parks and recreation programs in the state. Mahaney Park, the Mike Shellito Indoor Pool, and dozens of neighborhood parks keep residents active year round.

### Things to Do

The Westfield Galleria at Roseville is the premier shopping destination in the region. Fountains at Roseville adds even more retail and dining. But Roseville is more than malls. The Vernon Street Town Square hosts live music, food truck events, and holiday celebrations downtown.

Outdoor recreation is everywhere. The Miners Ravine Trail connects to a regional trail network perfect for biking and running. Folsom Lake is a short drive east.

### Schools and Parks

Roseville schools consistently perform above state averages. The city offers a blend of public, charter, and private options. Parks number over 80 across the city, with sports complexes, splash pads, and dog parks included. The Roseville Aquatics Complex hosts competitive swim meets and public swim sessions.
