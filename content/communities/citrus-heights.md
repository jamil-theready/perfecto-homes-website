## Citrus Heights: A Sacramento Suburb That Delivers

Citrus Heights sits just northeast of Sacramento, offering a suburban lifestyle without the long commute. The city covers about 14 square miles and is home to roughly 87,000 residents who enjoy tree lined streets, local parks, and easy access to shopping and dining along Sunrise Boulevard and Greenback Lane.

Families gravitate toward Citrus Heights for good reason. The cost of living runs lower than many neighboring communities, and the neighborhoods feel established. You get sidewalks, mature landscaping, and a genuine sense of community. Block parties still happen here. Neighbors still wave.

### Things to Do

Sunrise Mall anchors the retail scene, but the real gems are the local spots. Rusch Park hosts community events throughout the year, and the Sunrise Recreation and Park District runs programs for kids, adults, and seniors. The Stock Ranch area offers quick access to restaurants and entertainment along the Sunrise Corridor.

Outdoor lovers appreciate the proximity to Folsom Lake and the American River Parkway. A short drive puts you on trails, kayaks, or picnic blankets along the river.

### Schools and Parks

The San Juan Unified School District serves most of Citrus Heights. Families can choose from a range of public, charter, and private school options. Highly rated schools like Woodside K 8 and Mesa Verde High School draw families to specific neighborhoods.

Parks are everywhere. Rusch Park alone covers 50 acres with sports fields, a pool, and a community center. Tempo Park and Arcade Creek Park offer quieter green space for walks and picnics.
