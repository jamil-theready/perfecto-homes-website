## Folsom: Where History Meets Modern Suburban Living

Folsom blends Gold Rush heritage with a thoroughly modern lifestyle. The city sits along the American River east of Sacramento, known for its lake, its trails, and its family friendly neighborhoods. Folsom real estate listings attract buyers who want top schools, outdoor access, and a walkable historic district all in one city.

With a population just over 80,000, Folsom feels large enough to have everything you need and small enough to maintain a strong community identity.

### Why Families Pick Folsom

Schools drive a huge portion of home purchases here. The Folsom Cordova Unified School District includes some of the highest performing campuses in the Sacramento region. Folsom High School, Vista del Lago, and the newer school sites in Folsom Ranch consistently deliver strong academics and competitive athletics.

Safety is another factor. Folsom regularly ranks among the safest cities in California. Low crime rates, well maintained streets, and active neighborhood associations create an environment where families feel secure.

### Things to Do

Folsom Lake is the obvious headliner. Boating, swimming, fishing, and hiking around the lake keep residents busy all summer. The American River Parkway and the Johnny Cash Trail offer year round walking and biking paths.

Historic Sutter Street is the cultural center of town. Restaurants, boutique shops, and live music venues line the cobblestone corridor. Thursday Night Market runs during warmer months and draws visitors from across the region.

The Palladio at Broadstone provides upscale shopping and dining in an open air setting. Between Sutter Street and Palladio, Folsom covers all the bases.

### Schools

Folsom Cordova Unified School District serves the city and includes some of the highest performing campuses in the Sacramento region. The district keeps pace with Folsom's growth by opening new school sites in Folsom Ranch and Empire Ranch.

**Elementary Schools**

- Natoma Station Elementary. A top rated campus near the heart of Folsom. Strong parent involvement and high test scores.
- Folsom Hills Elementary. Serves the Broadstone area with a focus on STEM and literacy.
- Gold Ridge Elementary. Located in the Empire Ranch neighborhood. A newer school with modern facilities.
- Sandra J. Gallardo Elementary. One of the newest elementary campuses in Folsom Ranch. Built with the latest in classroom technology.
- Oak Chan Elementary. A well established school with a strong reputation in central Folsom.

**Middle Schools**

- Sutter Middle School. One of the most popular middle schools in the district. Strong honors program and active campus life.
- Folsom Middle School. Serves central Folsom with a solid academic foundation and competitive sports teams.
- Gold Ridge Middle School. A newer campus in the Empire Ranch area. Growing quickly with strong early results.
- Mitchell Middle School. Located in the Rancho Cordova border area but part of the Folsom Cordova district. Serves families on the west side.

**High Schools**

- Folsom High School. The flagship campus. Consistently ranks among the best public high schools in the Sacramento area. Strong AP classes, athletics, and school culture.
- Vista del Lago High School. Located in south Folsom near the lake. Known for excellent academics and a close knit community feel.
- Folsom Lake High School. An alternative education campus offering flexible scheduling for students who need a nontraditional path.

**Private and Charter Schools**

- Folsom Lake Christian Academy. A faith based private school offering K through 8 education with small class sizes.
- Montessori of Folsom. A private Montessori option for younger students focused on independent learning.
- Folsom Academy of Scholars. A charter school with a focus on academic rigor and college preparation.
- New Life Christian Academy. Another private option serving Folsom families with a values based curriculum.
- Heritage Oak Private Education. Offers personalized K through 8 instruction with a reputation for strong outcomes.

Folsom was designed with families in mind. Most neighborhoods in the city offer walkable or bikeable access to at least one school campus. Folsom Ranch is building new schools alongside new housing to keep pace with growth.

### Parks

Folsom maintains over 50 parks across the city. Livermore Community Park is the largest, featuring an aquatic center, sports fields, and picnic areas. Lembi Community Park and Cummins Park offer neighborhood level green space with playgrounds and walking paths.

Folsom Ranch is adding new parks as the area develops, including trail connections and community gathering spaces. The American River Parkway and Folsom Lake itself add thousands of acres of outdoor recreation within minutes of most neighborhoods.
