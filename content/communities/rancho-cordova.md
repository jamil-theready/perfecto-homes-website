## Rancho Cordova: Affordable Living East of Sacramento

Rancho Cordova has transformed over the past two decades from a quiet suburb into a thriving city with its own identity. Located along the Highway 50 corridor east of Sacramento, the city offers affordable housing, a growing job market, and a community that continues to invest in its future.

The area blends established neighborhoods with brand new developments. That mix gives buyers options whether they want a move in ready resale home or a freshly built property in a master planned community.

### Why Families Choose Rancho Cordova

Affordability tops the list. Rancho Cordova homes for sale consistently price below comparable properties in Folsom, Roseville, and even parts of Sacramento. Families get more home for their money here, and new construction keeps expanding the inventory.

The city has also invested heavily in parks, trails, and community programs. The Cordova Recreation and Park District runs youth sports, after school programs, and senior activities. Families feel supported here, and the infrastructure keeps improving.

### Things to Do

The American River runs along the northern border of Rancho Cordova, and residents take full advantage. Kayaking, fishing, and riverside picnics are weekend staples. The Mather Regional Park and Mather Field offer open space, trails, and community events.

Local dining has grown alongside the city. Restaurants and breweries along Folsom Boulevard serve a mix of cuisines. The annual Rancho Cordova Fourth of July celebration is one of the largest in the Sacramento region.

### Schools and Parks

Folsom Cordova Unified School District serves most of Rancho Cordova. Schools like Williamson Elementary and Cordova High School serve the community well. Newer developments in the Sunridge area have brought additional schools to keep pace with growth.

Parks are a standout feature. Hagan Community Park spans over 75 acres and hosts festivals, sports leagues, and the community pool. Smaller neighborhood parks are woven throughout the residential areas.
