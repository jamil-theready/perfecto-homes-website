## Elk Grove: One of Sacramento's Fastest Growing Cities

Elk Grove has grown from a small farming community into one of the largest cities in the Sacramento region. Located south of Sacramento along Highway 99, the city now has a population exceeding 176,000 and continues to expand with new neighborhoods, schools, and commercial development. Elk Grove homes for sale attract buyers who want modern housing, strong schools, and a family centered community.

The city earned recognition as one of the most diverse and safest cities in California. That combination of safety and diversity creates a welcoming environment where families from all backgrounds put down roots.

### Why Families Move to Elk Grove

Elk Grove Unified School District is one of the largest in the state and offers a wide range of academic programs. Families have access to traditional public schools, charter options, International Baccalaureate programs, and career technical education. Schools like Pleasant Grove High School, Franklin High School, and Laguna Creek High School serve the community well.

The city also delivers on recreation. The Elk Grove Aquatic Center, Bartholomew Sports Park, and the regional trail system give residents plenty of ways to stay active. Community events like the Elk Grove Western Festival and the Strauss Festival of the Arts bring neighbors together.

### Things to Do

Old Town Elk Grove serves as the historic and cultural heart of the city. Local shops, restaurants, and seasonal events keep the district lively. The Elk Grove Regional Park hosts weekend soccer games, birthday parties, and community gatherings.

Stone Lakes National Wildlife Refuge sits on the city's western edge, offering birdwatching and nature walks in a protected wetland environment. The Cosumnes River Preserve adds more outdoor options just south of town.

For everyday errands and entertainment, the Elk Grove Promenade and the expanding commercial corridors along Elk Grove Boulevard and Laguna Boulevard provide retail, dining, and services.

### Schools and Parks

Elk Grove Unified covers the city comprehensively. The district invests in facilities and programs that keep pace with the city's growth. Parks are abundant, with over 100 maintained by the Cosumnes Community Services District. Splash pads, sports complexes, and nature preserves are scattered throughout the city.
