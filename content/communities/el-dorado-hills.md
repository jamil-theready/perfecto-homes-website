## El Dorado Hills: Upscale Living in the Sacramento Foothills

El Dorado Hills sits in the rolling foothills of El Dorado County, just east of Folsom along Highway 50. The community is known for its higher end homes, top performing schools, and a quality of life that consistently ranks among the best in the Sacramento region. El Dorado Hills real estate draws buyers who want space, views, and access to outdoor recreation.

The Town Center serves as the community hub, offering shopping, dining, and a central gathering place that hosts seasonal events. The surrounding neighborhoods range from gated luxury estates to more accessible single family homes, giving buyers a spectrum of options.

### Why Families Choose El Dorado Hills

Schools are the primary driver. The Buckeye Union School District and the El Dorado Union High School District are among the highest rated in the state. Oak Ridge High School, Rolling Hills Middle School, and several award winning elementary campuses draw families from across the Sacramento metro area.

Beyond academics, the lifestyle here centers on the outdoors. Hiking and biking trails wind through the foothills. Folsom Lake sits minutes away. The elevation brings slightly cooler temperatures in summer and the occasional dusting of snow in winter. It feels like a retreat without being remote.

### Things to Do

El Dorado Hills Town Center is a walkable village with restaurants, boutiques, and a movie theater. Community events like outdoor concerts and holiday markets bring residents together throughout the year.

Folsom Lake and the surrounding state recreation area offer boating, swimming, mountain biking, and horseback riding. The El Dorado Trail provides a paved path for walking and cycling. For a day trip, Apple Hill is just up the hill and draws visitors every fall for apple picking and wine tasting.

### Schools and Parks

The school districts here rank among the top in California. Community parks include Kalithea Park, the El Dorado Hills Community Services District sports complex, and multiple neighborhood playgrounds. Youth sports programs are robust, with competitive leagues in soccer, baseball, basketball, and swimming.
