## Olympus Pointe Roseville: A Neighborhood Built for Families

Olympus Pointe is one of Roseville's most desirable residential communities. Tucked into the western part of the city near the intersection of Pleasant Grove Boulevard and Fiddyment Road, this master planned neighborhood delivers everything families want: strong schools, clean parks, and well built homes on quiet streets.

The community was developed with intentional design. Walking paths connect to neighborhood parks. Homes feature modern floor plans with open kitchens, three car garages, and energy efficient construction. The result is a neighborhood that feels polished without feeling sterile.

### Why Families Love Olympus Pointe

Schools are the anchor. Olympus Pointe feeds into some of the top rated campuses in the Roseville City School District and the Roseville Joint Union High School District. ### Things to Do Nearby

West Roseville offers excellent retail and dining within minutes of Olympus Pointe. The Blue Oaks Town Center provides grocery shopping, restaurants, and services. The Fountains at Roseville and the Westfield Galleria are a short drive east.

Outdoor recreation is close. The Reason Farms Park and other community green spaces sit within walking distance. Mahaney Park and the extensive Roseville trail system are easily accessible for biking, jogging, and dog walking.
