Located along the growing Highway 65 corridor, this neighborhood attracts families, professionals, and retirees who want modern homes with access to top schools, parks, and retail. If you are researching Olympus Pointe homes for sale, this guide covers everything you need to know before making a decision.</p>

<h3><strong>Overview of the Olympus Pointe Roseville Neighborhood</strong></h3>

<p>Olympus Pointe is a master planned development in the western portion of Roseville, situated between Pleasant Grove Boulevard and Blue Oaks Boulevard. The community was developed over several phases, with homes built primarily from the mid 2010s through the early 2020s. That means most of the housing stock here is relatively new, with modern floor plans, energy efficient systems, and current building standards.</p>

<p>The neighborhood is well organized with wide streets, sidewalks on both sides, and a network of walking paths that connect to parks and common areas. It is the kind of community where families walk to school and neighbors cross paths on evening strolls.</p>

<h3><strong>Home Styles and Price Ranges</strong></h3>

<p>Olympus Pointe homes for sale include a range of options built by several production builders. You will find single story and two story floor plans across the community. Most homes feature open concept living areas, attached two or three car garages, and private backyards.</p>

<ul>
<li>Smaller homes with three bedrooms and around 1,600 to 1,900 square feet start in the mid $500,000s</li>
<li>Four bedroom family homes between 2,200 and 2,800 square feet typically list in the $600,000 to $750,000 range</li>
<li>Larger five bedroom homes and premium lots can reach $800,000 or more</li>
</ul>

<p>The newer construction means lower maintenance costs in the early years of ownership. Solar panels, tankless water heaters, and smart thermostats come standard in many of the homes built in the later phases.</p>

<h3><strong>School Assignments</strong></h3>

<p>Families in Olympus Pointe benefit from Roseville's strong public school system. Most students in this neighborhood are assigned to schools within the Roseville City School District for elementary and middle school. Specific assignments depend on your address, but the district as a whole performs well above state averages.</p>

<p>For high school, students typically attend Woodcreek High School in the Roseville Joint Union High School District. Woodcreek offers a full slate of AP courses, athletics, and performing arts programs. The district also provides school choice options that give families some flexibility.</p>

<h3><strong>HOA Details</strong></h3>

<p>Olympus Pointe has an active homeowners association that manages common areas, landscaping, and community amenities. HOA dues vary by sub association within the community but generally fall in the range of $80 to $140 per month.</p>

<p>The HOA enforces CC&Rs that cover exterior paint colors, landscaping standards, parking rules, and noise guidelines. These rules help maintain property values and keep the neighborhood looking consistent. Buyers should review the CC&Rs carefully before purchasing to make sure the guidelines align with their expectations.</p>

<h3><strong>Parks and Recreation</strong></h3>

<p>Green space is a defining feature of the Olympus Pointe Roseville neighborhood. The community includes multiple pocket parks, playgrounds, and open lawn areas distributed throughout the development. Walking trails connect different sections of the neighborhood and provide safe routes for joggers and dog walkers.</p>

<p>The larger parks in the area feature basketball courts, picnic shelters, and tot lots. West Roseville in general has benefited from significant investment in public parks and recreation infrastructure over the past decade. Mahaney Park and other regional facilities are a short drive away.</p>

<h3><strong>Blue Oaks Town Center Proximity</strong></h3>

<p>One of the biggest advantages of living in Olympus Pointe is the proximity to Blue Oaks Town Center. This retail hub sits just minutes from the neighborhood and includes grocery stores, restaurants, a fitness center, medical offices, and a variety of service businesses.</p>

<p>You can handle most daily errands without leaving the immediate area. Target, Raley's, and several fast casual dining spots anchor the center. Additional retail and commercial development continues along the Blue Oaks corridor, which adds convenience and supports property values in the surrounding neighborhoods.</p>

<h3><strong>Commute and Location</strong></h3>

<p>Olympus Pointe is positioned with easy access to Highway 65 and Interstate 80. Downtown Sacramento is approximately 25 to 30 minutes by car depending on traffic. The Roseville Galleria, Topgolf, and the Placer County government center are all within a 10 to 15 minute drive.</p>

<p>For remote workers, the fast internet infrastructure in newer Roseville developments is a practical bonus. Most homes in Olympus Pointe have access to fiber optic service, which supports video calls and large file transfers without issues.</p>

<p>The Olympus Pointe Roseville neighborhood is a strong fit for buyers who want newer construction, good schools, and a well maintained community without the price tag of some of the more established Roseville neighborhoods.
